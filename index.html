What “EPA” means here

FGC EPA estimates the points a team contributes to a three-team regional alliance. It fits every played ranking match simultaneously and shrinks early estimates toward the event average.

It is intentionally separate from the official ranking score. The official rank answers who is advancing; EPA tries to answer who is actually scoring.

This is an independent analytics project, not an official FIRST Global metric.
